FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a project cost?

Projects start from R 3 000. Because every business is different, I quote each project individually. Tell me what you need and I'll send a clear, itemised proposal that fits your budget, with no obligation.

How long will it take?

It depends on what you need. We agree on a timeline together during Discovery, and I keep you updated at every step so there are no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside South Africa?

Yes. I'm based in Cape Town and happy to work with clients across Africa and around the world. Everything can be handled remotely.

What happens after my site or system goes live?

I handle testing, deployment and handover, and include training and support. I also offer managed hosting, SSL and ongoing maintenance if you'd like me to look after things.

I don't have a logo or brand yet. Can you help?

Absolutely. My Brand & UI/UX service covers logos, colour palettes, typography and brand assets, so your new site launches with a consistent, professional look.
