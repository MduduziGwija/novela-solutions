FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted individually based on scope. Our projects range from under R 5 000 for a small site to R 50 000+ for larger business systems. Share a few details and we'll send you a clear proposal.

How long will it take?

It depends on what you need. We agree on a timeline with you during Discovery and keep you updated at every step, so there are no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside South Africa?

Yes. We're based in Cape Town and serve clients across Africa and around the world — everything can be handled remotely.

What happens after my site or system goes live?

We handle testing, deployment and handover, and include training and support. We also offer managed hosting, SSL and ongoing maintenance if you'd like us to look after things.

I don't have a logo or brand yet — can you help?

Absolutely. Our Brand & UI/UX service covers logos, colour palettes, typography and brand assets, so your new site launches with a consistent, professional look.
